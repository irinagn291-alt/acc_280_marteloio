Three behaviours are deliberate.

1. Hail stays inactive until Pages read is a whole number above zero. Type a page count, then tap Hail. An unusable value shows Enter a whole number above zero.

2. A second Hail is refused while a Run is still open. The sheet says A Run is still open. Bear it before you Hail again. Close it and tap a genre card marked Spend.

3. The card marked Open does not spend pages. It opens Spend pages as distance. Cards marked Later are too far; a tap writes a Luff and keeps the Run. Place pages only on a card that shows Spend.
